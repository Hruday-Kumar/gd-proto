# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students joining study and collaboration rooms. They are likely in an academic context but need a reliable, efficient way to connect with peers for studying or matchmaking.

## Product Purpose
To provide an app UI for joining rooms, matchmaking, and viewing history. Success looks like students quickly and seamlessly finding and joining the right study rooms without friction.

## Brand Personality
Professional, serious, secure.

## Anti-references
Don't make it look like a boring academic portal. It should feel professional and secure without feeling outdated or institutional.

## Design Principles
- **Professional clarity**: Prioritize clear navigation and workflow completion over playful academic tropes.
- **Secure reliability**: Build trust through consistent, fast, and bug-free interactions.
- **Modern efficiency**: Avoid the clunky, outdated feel of typical academic portals by using precise layouts and typography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific requirements noted, but adhere to baseline best practices (WCAG contrast, semantic HTML).
